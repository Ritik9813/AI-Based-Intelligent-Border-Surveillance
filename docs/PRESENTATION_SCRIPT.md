# 6-minute demo script
1. **Problem (30 s).** Long, remote borders; too many alarms; slow response.
2. **Sign in (15 s).** `run.bat` or `./run.sh`; admin / admin. Point out roles and the audit log.
3. **Upload (60 s).** *Upload video* on CAM-01. Boxes and confidence appear. Mention the neural detector.
4. **Zones (45 s).** Live Cameras > *Draw restricted zones*; draw a polygon on the fence area; save.
5. **Events (75 s).** New events appear with risk levels. Open one: evidence frame, **replay clip**, confidence, channels. Show the map and the donut chart.
6. **Control (60 s).** Dispatch a High event without acknowledging: refused, "Policy blocks" rises. Acknowledge, then dispatch. Show who did it in the AI Agent audit log.
7. **Assistant and reports (45 s).** Ask "show high risk events" and "explain #1001". Download the CSV / open the printable report.
8. **Analytics (30 s).** Heatmaps and camera health. Settings: trails, heat overlay, webhook, users.
9. **Close (20 s).** State the limits: general COCO model, no drone class, test on real footage, human stays in charge.
